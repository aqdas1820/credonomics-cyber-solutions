# Code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive and welcoming. Discuss technical disagreements
with evidence and focus on the work. Harassment, discrimination, threats,
personal attacks and disclosure of another person's private information are
not acceptable in project spaces.

Report concerns privately to support@credonomics.in. Maintainers may remove
harmful content, warn participants or restrict participation as appropriate.
They should apply these expectations fairly and respect the privacy of
reports. This policy applies to issues, pull requests and other official
project discussions.
